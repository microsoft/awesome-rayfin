# NOTICE: credits, data sources and licences

Caldova Cologne Digital Twin renders a real place from official open data and runs a fictional
company inside it. Every source below is attributed as its licence requires. **Read this before
publishing anything built from this template.**

Nothing in `public/terrain/` is committed: it is generated by `npm run data:build` from the sources
below. The two baked snapshots in `public/data/` are committed; they hold synthetic figures only.

### Geobasis NRW

> Datenquelle: Land NRW (Geobasis NRW), dl-de/zero-2-0

dl-de/zero-2-0 requires no attribution; it is given anyway, because a map that will not say where its
ground came from is worth less than one that does.

The photogrammetric 3D-Mesh is the product `Mesh_KB1450T12_nw_2025` (Bildflug 2025-05-11),
published by Geobasis NRW as one SLPK (I3S scene layer package) at
`https://www.opengeodata.nrw.de/produkte/geobasis/lusat/akt/3dmesh/3dmesh/`. The build does not
download the package: it reads the package's directory once and fetches only the selected nodes with
HTTP Range requests, then clips them, downsamples their textures and re-encodes them as GLB tiles
(`public/terrain/koeln-messe/mesh/`, `mesh-ring/`). dl-de/zero-2-0 permits this processing without
conditions; the tiles are still labelled with their source in `mesh.json` and in the app.

## Fictional and synthetic content

- **Caldova is a fictional company.** Its use of the Confex building at Koelnmesse is fictional. The
  building, its footprint and height, the terrain, the imagery and the roads are measured.
- **Pharmacy names are fictional** ("Caldova Partner Pharmacy 017"); the locations are real
  pharmacies from OpenStreetMap. Nothing in the app claims that any real pharmacy is a customer of
  any wholesaler.
- The logistics centre interior, machines, volumes, tours, stock, sales, reorders and all finance
  figures are generated by seeded scripts in `tools/data/` and badged as synthetic in the UI.
- No Koelnmesse logos or trademarks are used. Koelnmesse has not supplied, reviewed or endorsed
  anything in this template.
