This is Campus Insights, a photoreal three-D campus twin running as a Microsoft Fabric App.

Everything you can see here is measured. The terrain is one-metre laser survey. The ground is a twenty-centimetre orthophoto. Every building comes from the official cadastre, and every tree is a real surveyed tree.

This is T U M Garching. Click a building, and it opens.

Three thousand nine hundred real rooms, mapped in OpenStreetMap, joined to actual TUMonline lecture bookings. Three hundred and ten of them publish a calendar, and a hundred and thirty four were never booked once, all semester. Bookable, and completely unused.

Campus Flow routes real lecture cohorts along real footpaths, to show where the campus actually gets crowded.

Now switch site. The same engine draws Tübingen from a different survey authority entirely. A hundred and twenty seven metres of relief, six thousand medieval buildings, and the Neckar.

Here the question is the renovation backlog. Those figures are synthetic, and the app says so permanently, right above the numbers.

Measured where it can be. Labelled where it cannot.
