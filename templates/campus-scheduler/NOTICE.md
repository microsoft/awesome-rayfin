# NOTICE — data sources and licences

Campus Scheduler renders real places from official open data. Every source below is
attributed as its licence requires. **Read this before publishing anything built from
this repository.**

Nothing in `public/terrain/` is committed: it is generated by
`tools/geodata/pipeline.py` from the sources below.

⚠️ **THE SURVEY AUTHORITY IS A PROPERTY OF THE SITE, NOT OF THE PROJECT.** Eight areas of
interest ship, across four German state surveys, and the notice each one requires is
different. Getting this wrong is not hypothetical here: `build_lod2_mesh.py` once stamped the
**Bavarian** LDBV notice onto Tübingen, which is Baden-Württemberg's. Each AOI declares its own
text in `config/aoi/<id>.json` under `geobasis.attribution` and `shellGeobasis.attribution`, the
app renders that string rather than a constant, and
`src/config/__tests__/notice.test.ts` fails if any shipped AOI names an authority this file does
not credit. **Add a site, and this file must grow with it.**

## Room semantics and lecture bookings — NavigaTUM / TUMonline

**NavigaTUM** — <https://github.com/TUM-Dev/NavigaTUM> — software under GPL-3.0.
Room and calendar data originate from **TUMonline**.

> ⚠️ **NOT REDISTRIBUTED. This is a switch, not a warning.**
> The GPL-3.0 licence covers the NavigaTUM *software*. The redistribution terms of the
> underlying TUMonline *data* are not established, so internal demonstration is fine and a
> published build carrying a baked semester snapshot is not.
>
> `config/release.json` decides what Garching's interiors are made of, and one file drives both
> the app and the geodata pipeline so the two cannot disagree about what ships:
>
> | `navigatumData` | Effect |
> |---|---|
> | `"include"` | Real NavigaTUM semantics and real TUMonline bookings. The internal demo. **Not publishable.** |
> | `"synthetic"` | **Publishable, and the explode view survives.** The room *polygons* are OpenStreetMap's under ODbL and were never TUM's to withhold, so a building still opens into real floors and real rooms. Usage is read from the OSM `room` tag where it exists and inferred from floor area otherwise; the teaching week is invented, seeded per room code so it is identical on every rebuild. **No course titles are generated** — a made-up lecture name on a real, checkable room number is a false statement about a real place, which a badged percentage is not. The flow lens is still withheld: it is routed from real consecutive bookings, and inventing where people walk is a bigger claim than inventing a utilisation figure. |
> | `"exclude"` | No interiors at all. Garching keeps terrain, orthophoto, buildings and trees — all LDBV CC BY 4.0 and OpenStreetMap ODbL — and loses rooms, occupancy, flow and the planner. |
>
> `"excludeAois": ["garching"]` is the separate, blunter lever: the site does not ship at all. It
> leaves the switcher, its dot on the national map stops offering to open it, `?aoi=garching`
> falls back to the default, and the pipeline refuses to build it.
>
> **The planner goes in both withholding modes.** Its dataset is a separate derivation of the same
> TUMonline bookings, in `data/tum/`; substituting the twin's occupancy does not substitute that.
>
> Verify before publishing:
>
> ```
> npm run check:release        # posture + whether the assets on disk match it (exit 1 if not)
> npm run test:release-switch  # runs the unit suite in all six postures
> ```
>
> ⚠️ **`check:release` reads the `semantics` stamp inside `rooms.json`, not the filename.** A real
> and a synthetic build produce identically named files; only the stamp distinguishes them, and a
> leftover from an internal build is exactly how TUM data would reach a public release.
>
> ⚠️ **No setting here edits tracked files.** `/data/` and `/public/terrain/` are gitignored, so no
> NavigaTUM response or calendar snapshot has ever been committed — but `config/aoi/garching.json`
> carries `navigatumRooms` counts read from the API. For a repository with no TUM-derived values
> at all, `npm run check:release` lists the files to delete.

NavigaTUM is run by students and carries no SLA. The pipeline caches aggressively and the
app never calls it at page load (PLAN D4).

## Timetable data — a university's own scheduling system (Untis)

`tools/data/read_untis_gpu.py` turns a real Untis export (GPU001/GPU002/GPU005) into
`data/oth-real/`, so the planner can be evaluated against a real week rather than a generated one.

> ⚠️ **NOT REDISTRIBUTED, AND THE RESTRICTION IS STRONGER THAN THE TUM ONE.**
> TUM's bookings are at least published somewhere. This kind of export is not: it is one
> institution's internal room, lecturer and class data, and none of it is public in any form.
>
> `config/release.json` → `realCustomerData` decides. With it withheld (the default, and anything
> other than the literal `"include"`), the OTH AOI falls back to the **generated** site `oth` —
> the same campus, the same twin, an invented week that `tools/data/generate_timetable.py`
> reproduces from `config/academic/oth.json` — and `?scheduler=oth-real` is ignored rather than
> pointed at a backend that is not there. Nothing the planner demonstrates is lost.
>
> What the export legitimately left behind is *knowledge*, not rows, and that stays: the block
> scheme in `config/academic/oth.json` is 6 × 90 min from 08:15 c.t. **because their export
> disproved our assumption of 7 from 08:00**, and the slot desirability curve is shaped like the
> real distribution of teaching across the week rather than like a guess. Neither is a booking.

## Synthetic data

Some figures in this app are **invented**. They are generated by committed, seeded rules
and are badged as `synthetisch` wherever they appear. They are not statements about any
real institution:

- seat counts per room (derived from real floor area × a planning density)
- attendance and cohort sizes
- building condition grades (Zustandsnoten), refurbishment cost and investment scenarios
- **wall colour.** A wall is not visible in a vertical aerial photograph, so unlike the
  roofs it cannot be measured. What *is* measured is what each building **is** — its
  ALKIS `bldg:function` code, its own footprint and height, and, for the universities'
  own buildings, the OpenStreetMap `operator` tag. The colour each of those classes is
  painted is a convention chosen to match Bavarian urban practice, and is not a claim
  about any individual wall.

Real, measured data: terrain, orthophotos, buildings, vegetation, room polygons, room
usage types, floor assignment and lecture bookings. Room **area** is derived by
computation from real polygons. The full table is in the README under
*What is real and what is not*, and each dataset carries its own machine-readable
`provenance` block — `data/<site>/provenance.json` and the `provenance` key inside
`public/terrain/<aoi>/rooms.json`.

**Roof colour is measured, and it is measured from the orthophoto.** Each building's
roof takes the median of the DOP20 drape pixels inside its own LoD2 roof outlines —
98.0% of buildings at Regensburg, 99.2% at Munich; the rest fall back to the site
median and are flagged as such in `buildings_colour.bin`. Green-dominant pixels are
rejected so overhanging trees do not turn roofs into lawns, and the sample is
re-centred in brightness to remove the sun the photograph was taken in, because the
renderer adds its own light and would otherwise shade everything twice.

⚠️ **A photograph is not a survey of materials.** The roof colour is the roof as it
looked on the morning of one flight. A roof re-tiled since then shows as it was.
