The Awesome Rayfin copy adds gallery metadata, a gallery manifest, template
identifiers and template-specific setup instructions. Those changes are kept
in `microsoft/awesome-rayfin`; they do not change the upstream Fabric Atlas
repository.

Update this template by importing a reviewed Fabric Atlas commit, reapplying
the gallery overlay, running the gallery generator and validating the complete
scaffold again.
